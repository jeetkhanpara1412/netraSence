# Our Unique Innovation

### **Self-Aware Explainable AI for Rural DR Screening**

Our system will go beyond normal Diabetic Retinopathy classification.

**Key innovation:**

> **The AI will not only predict DR, but also explain its decision, measure its own uncertainty, verify whether its explanation is reliable, and decide when a case must be reviewed by an ophthalmologist.**

### Workflow

**Retinal Image**
↓
**Quality Check & Enhancement**
↓
**DR Level 0–4 Prediction**
↓
**Lesion Detection**
↓
**Grad-CAM Explanation**
↓
**Confidence & Uncertainty Check**
↓
**AI Self-Validation**
↓
**Risk-Based Patient Triage**
↓
**Ophthalmologist Review**

### Why it is valuable

* Reduces **black-box AI** problems.
* Helps doctors understand **why AI made the decision**.
* Detects **uncertain or unreliable predictions**.
* Prioritizes **high-risk patients** when doctors are limited.
* Designed specifically for **poor-quality images and rural healthcare conditions**.
* Can be combined with **Simulink resource simulation** for large-scale deployment.

### Our Core Idea

> **“An AI that knows what it sees, explains why it decided, knows when it may be wrong, and sends uncertain cases to a human expert.”**

**Important:** We should present this as a **proposed innovation**, not claim that no similar system exists anywhere in the world without conducting a global literature/product review.
